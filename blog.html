Halfway Acquired by Akagawa Innovations Inc.

July 16, 2025 Announcement

I'm excited to announce that Halfway has been acquired by Akagawa Innovations Inc. in a 75% stake deal, with Zecro Studios retaining 25%. The contract was signed on July 16, 2025.

This partnership opens up exciting possibilities for Halfway! We have incredible updates planned - a complete app overhaul, powerful new features, and exciting integrations that will transform how people discover and plan meetups. Akagawa Innovations Inc. aligns perfectly with taking Halfway to the next level.

We're working on something truly amazing. Our focus remains on the core mission of making meetups with friends and people easier.
