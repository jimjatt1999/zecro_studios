Daily History

iOS

Discover history that connects to today.

Get AI-powered insights into the events that shaped our world, delivered fresh to your phone every morning.

Premium AI Features:

  • AI Deep Analysis: Go beyond Wikipedia with expert insights and hidden connections behind any historical event.
  • Chat with Historical Figures: Have authentic conversations with Napoleon, Socrates, Leonardo da Vinci, and other fascinating personalities from history.

Core Features:

  • Today Widget: History at a glance on your Home Screen
  • Interactive World Map: Visualize where history happened across continents
  • Stories Mode: Swipe through history like social media
  • Personal Collection: Save favorite events and create beautiful sharing cards
  • Smart Features: Wikipedia integration, era filters, and offline reading

Perfect for: Students, curious minds, history enthusiasts, and anyone wanting to understand current events through historical context.

Transform your daily routine into a journey through time. Chat with the greatest minds in history and gain insights that connect the past to today.

Built for iOS 18+ with Apple's latest liquid glass design language.
