HI(STORIES)

iOS

Discover History's Most Fascinating Moments.

Step into the past with HI(STORIES) - your daily gateway to the most captivating historical events that happened on this very day throughout history. From ancient civilizations to modern breakthroughs, explore births, deaths, and pivotal moments that shaped our world.

Core Features:

  • Daily Discovery: Uncover fascinating historical events for any date
  • Beautiful Timeline: Navigate through history with an elegant, intuitive interface
  • Rich Visuals: Stunning imagery brings historical moments to life
  • Personal Collection: Save your favorite events with a simple tap
  • Share History: Create beautiful Instagram-ready cards to share amazing historical facts
  • Stories Mode: Swipe through history like your favorite social feed

Whether you're a history buff or simply curious about the past, HI(STORIES) transforms learning into an engaging daily ritual. Perfect for educators, students, or anyone who loves discovering the incredible stories that connect us across time.

Built for iOS 18+ to take advantage of the new Apple liquid glass design. Optimized for the latest iOS features.

Turn every day into a journey through time.
