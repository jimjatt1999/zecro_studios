Daily History

iOS

Discover history that connects to today.

Get AI-powered insights into the events that shaped our world, delivered fresh to your phone every morning.

Premium AI Features:

Unlock the full power of artificial intelligence to explore history like never before.

  • AI Deep Analysis: Go beyond Wikipedia. Uncover hidden connections, expert insights, and lasting impact of any historical event with advanced AI analysis.
  • Chat with Historical Figures: Have real conversations with history's most fascinating personalities. Ask Napoleon about his campaigns, discuss philosophy with Socrates, or explore art with Leonardo da Vinci. Our AI brings historical figures to life for authentic educational conversations.

Core Features:

  • Today Widget: History at a glance on your Home Screen. Never miss significant events from this day in history.
  • Interactive World Map: Visualize where history happened. Explore events by location and see how they connect across continents.
  • Stories Mode: Swipe through history like social media. Discover daily events in an engaging, visual format.

Perfect For:

  • Students studying for history classes
  • Curious minds who love learning
  • Anyone wanting to understand current events through historical context
  • History enthusiasts building knowledge

Key Features:

  • Daily historical feed with curated events
  • Personal collection to save favorite events
  • Beautiful sharing cards for social media
  • Seamless Wikipedia integration
  • Smart filters by era and event type
  • Offline reading capability

Transform your daily routine into a journey through time. Chat with the greatest minds in history and gain insights that connect the past to today. Download now and make every day a learning adventure.

Built for iOS 18+ to take advantage of the new Apple liquid glass design. Optimized for the latest iOS features.
