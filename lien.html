Lien

iOS

Nurture Your Meaningful Connections.

In today's busy world, it's easy to let important relationships drift. Lien provides the tools to be more intentional with your connections. Keep track of interactions with friends, family, and colleagues, remember key details, get timely reminders to reach out, and gain insights to maintain and strengthen the bonds that matter most.

Core Features:

  • People Management: Add, edit, and organize important people.
  • Interaction Logging: Record meetings, calls, messages with notes, moods, locations, photos.
  • Timeline View: See birthdays, anniversaries, and suggested reconnection times.
  • Home Dashboard: Quick overview with connection suggestions & prompts.
  • Contextual Prompts: Smart conversation starters based on context & details.
  • 'Remember This' Snippets: Store important details about individuals.
  • Interaction Insights: View stats on frequency, type, recency, and mood.
  • Relationship Linking: Group contacts (family, work team).
  • Life Events: Track significant milestones.
