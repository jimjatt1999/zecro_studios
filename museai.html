Muse AI

iOS iPadOS macOS

An AI app to organize your thoughts.

Muse transforms your spoken thoughts into beautifully organized notes using cutting-edge on-device AI. Record your voice and watch as Muse transcribes, enhances, and organizes your thoughts - all while maintaining complete privacy with no internet connection required.

Key Features:

  • On-Device AI Transcription: Accurate voice-to-text using OpenAI's Whisper tiny model with complete privacy.
  • AI-Enhanced Notes: Automatic organization, grammar improvements, and smart title suggestions.
  • Multiple Note Types: Normal Notes, Journal Entries, and Meeting Notes with intelligent categorization.
  • Intuitive UX: Clean interface with animated eye indicators showing processing status.
  • Personalization: Custom profile, theme colors, and appearance settings.
  • Privacy-First: All processing happens on your device with no data sent to external servers.
  • Smart Organization: Date-based grouping, full-text search, and filtering by note type.
