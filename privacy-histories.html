DAILY HISTORY PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 2024

Your Privacy is Our Priority

Daily History is designed with privacy at its core. We believe your learning journey and personal interests should remain private and secure.

Data We Do NOT Collect or Transmit

Daily History operates with minimal data collection. We do not collect, store externally, transmit, or have access to your personal usage data, including but not limited to:

  • Your saved historical events or personal collections.
  • Your browsing patterns or which historical events you view most frequently.
  • Your sharing activity or social media interactions.
  • Personal notes or comments you might add to historical events.
  • Your location data (the app does not request location permissions).
  • Any personal information or account details.

Your learning activity stays on your device.

Exception: When you use the optional AI chat feature, your chat messages are transmitted to OpenAI for processing. See the "AI Chat Feature and OpenAI Integration" section below for detailed information about this data sharing.

Data Storage

Daily History stores minimal data locally on your device, such as your saved favorite events and app preferences. This data is included in your regular device backups (like iCloud Backup) if you have them enabled, according to your Apple settings, but it is not sent to us or any third party.

Historical Content

The historical events, images, and content displayed in Daily History are sourced from public historical records and educational resources. This content is provided for educational purposes and does not contain any personal information about our users.

AI Chat Feature and OpenAI Integration

Important Disclosure: Daily History includes an AI-powered chat feature that allows you to ask questions about historical events and topics. This feature is powered by OpenAI's API services.

What happens when you use the AI chat:

  • Data transmission: Your chat messages and questions are sent to OpenAI's servers for processing.
  • OpenAI's data policy: According to OpenAI's current policies, your API data is not used to train their models.
  • Data retention: OpenAI may retain your chat messages for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring and system improvement purposes.
  • Our storage: We do not store or save your AI chat conversations on our servers or your device.
  • Privacy consideration: Avoid sharing personal, sensitive, or private information in AI chat conversations.

By using the AI chat feature, you acknowledge that your messages will be processed by OpenAI according to their privacy policy and terms of service. You can find OpenAI's privacy policy at: https://openai.com/privacy/

The AI chat feature is optional, and you can enjoy all other features of Daily History without using it.

Other Third-Party Services

Daily History may also use other third-party services for:

  • Retrieving historical content and images from educational databases
  • App analytics to improve performance (anonymized data only)
  • Crash reporting to fix bugs (no personal data included)

These services do not have access to your personal usage patterns or saved content.

Sharing Features

When you use the sharing feature to create Instagram-ready cards or share historical facts, the sharing is handled entirely by your device's built-in sharing capabilities. We do not track or store information about what you share or with whom.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy if future app features necessitate changes. Our fundamental commitment to protecting your privacy and keeping your learning activity private will remain. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or on this website.
