Privacy Policy - Lien App

Your Privacy Comes First

Lien is designed from the ground up with your privacy as the top priority. We believe your personal relationships and the details you track are yours alone.

Data We Do NOT Collect or Transmit

Lien operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, store externally, transmit, or have access to any of the personal data you enter into the app, including but not limited to:

  • Names or contact information of people you add.
  • Details of your logged interactions (notes, dates, locations, moods, photos).
  • Birthdays, anniversaries, or other life events.
  • 'Remember This' snippets or other notes.
  • Data derived from insights or statistics generated within the app.
  • Your location data (unless you explicitly add it to an interaction note).
  • Any data accessed from Contacts or Calendar if permissions are granted (processing happens on-device only if such features are implemented).

Simply put: Your data stays on your device. We cannot see it.

Data Storage

All the information you enter into Lien is stored locally and securely on your device using Apple's standard data storage frameworks. This data is included in your regular device backups (like iCloud Backup) if you have them enabled, according to your Apple settings, but it is not sent to us or any third party.

Permissions

Future versions of Lien might request permissions (e.g., Contacts, Calendar, Location, Photos) to enhance functionality. If requested:

  • These permissions will be optional.
  • Any data accessed via these permissions will be processed solely on your device to provide the intended feature (e.g., showing birthdays from Contacts, adding a photo to an interaction).
  • Accessed data will not be stored separately by Lien unless necessary for the feature (e.g., linking to a contact) and will never be transmitted off your device by Lien.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy if future app features necessitate changes in how local data is handled. Our fundamental commitment to not collecting or transmitting your personal relationship data will remain. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or on this website.
