Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2024

Your Thoughts, Your Privacy, Your Device

Welcome to Muse AI. This app is designed with your privacy as the highest priority. We believe you should have access to powerful AI transcription and organization tools without compromising your personal data. This policy explains how Muse AI handles your data – or rather, how it doesn't.

Data We Do NOT Collect

Muse AI operates 100% on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any of the following personal data:

  • Your voice recordings.
  • Your transcribed text.
  • Notes created within the app.
  • Personal identifiers (name, profile photo, etc.).
  • Your device information.
  • Usage analytics or behavior patterns.
  • Your location data.

Everything happens locally on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

Data Processed On-Device

Muse AI processes the following data locally on your device to provide its features:

  • Audio Recordings: When you record your voice, the audio is processed locally by OpenAI's Whisper tiny model running directly on your device.
  • Transcribed Text: The text generated from your recordings is processed locally by on-device AI models to enhance and organize it.
  • Note Organization: The app categorizes your notes as Normal Notes, Journal Entries, or Meeting Notes based on content analysis that occurs entirely on your device.
  • Profile Information: Any name or photo you add to personalize the app is stored locally and never transmitted.

This processing occurs entirely within the secure environment of your device. No data is sent to external servers.

Permissions

  • Microphone Access: Required for recording your voice. Audio is processed locally and never leaves your device.
  • Photo Library Access: Optional, required only if you choose to add a profile photo. The photo is stored locally.
  • Notifications: Optional, for reminders about notes or app updates.

You can manage these permissions in your device's Settings.

Data Storage

Your recordings, transcriptions, and notes are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure frameworks. This data is included in your standard device backups (iCloud or iTunes/Finder) if you have those enabled, but it is never accessible to us.

Children's Privacy

Muse AI does not knowingly collect any data from users, including children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy if future features require changes in data handling, although our core commitment to on-device processing and privacy will remain. We will notify you of significant changes by updating this policy within the app or on our website.
