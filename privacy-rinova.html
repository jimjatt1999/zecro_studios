RINOVA PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: December 2025

Purpose

Rinova is a personal spending tracker with multi-account, multi-currency support, offline-first storage, and optional currency conversion. Your finances remain private and under your control.

Data We Do NOT Collect or Send

Rinova operates locally. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any of the financial data you enter into the app, including:

  • Account names, balances, currencies, or icons.
  • Transactions (amounts, notes, dates, categories, attachments).
  • Budgets, reminders, or settings.
  • Charts, insights, or any derived analytics.

Your data stays on your device. We cannot access it.

Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using iOS storage. It is included in your normal device backups (e.g., iCloud Backup) if you have them enabled, according to your Apple settings.

Currency Conversion

To convert between currencies, Rinova fetches public exchange rates. These requests do not include personal data—only the currency codes needed to retrieve rates. Rates are cached so the app continues working offline.

Permissions

Rinova may ask for notifications (for reminders). No other permissions are required. If future features request additional permissions, they will remain optional and explained in-app.

Children's Privacy

Rinova is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy as features evolve. Significant changes will be posted in the app or on this site.
