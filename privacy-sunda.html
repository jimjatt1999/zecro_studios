SUNDA PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how Sunda, an iOS focus and digital-wellbeing app provided by Zecro Studios ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information.

Summary

Sunda does not require an account and does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data with Zecro Studios. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics. Sunda's focus features use Apple's privacy-preserving Screen Time frameworks and store app settings locally on your device.

Information Sunda Handles on Your Device

When you use Sunda, the app may store your focus rules, schedules, check-in preferences, session state, reminder settings, and onboarding progress locally on your device. Sunda and its extensions share this state through an Apple App Group container so that blocks, widgets, reports, and reminders can work together.

Screen Time and Family Controls

If you grant Screen Time access, Sunda uses Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings frameworks to let you select apps, categories, or websites; apply protections; and display usage patterns.

Selections are represented using opaque tokens supplied by Apple. Sunda does not need to learn the underlying bundle identifiers or names of selected apps. Device Activity reports are processed within Apple's Screen Time framework and displayed in the app. Zecro Studios does not receive your selections, usage history, pickups, or Screen Time reports.

Notifications

With your permission, Sunda can schedule local notifications for reminders and focus events. Notification content and schedules are created on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Audio

Sunda includes optional meditation audio and generated water sounds that play on your device. The app does not record audio and does not request microphone access.

Shortcuts and Widgets

If you choose to use Sunda's App Shortcuts or widgets, Apple may process those interactions as part of iOS. Sunda uses them only to run the focus actions you request and does not transmit their contents to Zecro Studios.

Data Collection, Tracking, and Third Parties

Sunda does not operate a backend service, use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or collect diagnostics on behalf of Zecro Studios. Apple may process App Store, device, crash, or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple's own privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Sunda's settings are stored locally, you control them. You can remove rules and schedules inside the app. Deleting Sunda removes its locally stored app data, subject to Apple's device-backup and system-retention behaviour.

Children's Privacy

Sunda is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because Zecro Studios does not collect personal data through Sunda, we do not knowingly retain personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if Sunda's features or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
