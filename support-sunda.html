SUPPORT // SUNDA

HOW CAN WE HELP?

For technical issues, accessibility questions, feedback, or feature requests, email jimjatt1999@gmail.com.

When reporting a technical issue, please include your Sunda version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send screenshots containing sensitive information.

Getting Started

  • Open Sunda and allow Screen Time access when asked.
  • Create a rule and use Apple's picker to choose the apps, categories, or websites you want Sunda to help with.
  • Choose a pause style and save the rule.
  • For scheduled protection, create a recurring block from the Blocks tab.

Why Screen Time Permission Is Needed

Sunda uses Apple's Family Controls and Device Activity frameworks to apply the rules you create and show usage patterns. Without Screen Time permission, app protection and Screen Time reports cannot operate.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and are used for reminders and focus events. You can change permission at any time in iOS Settings > Notifications > Sunda.

Your Privacy

Sunda requires no account and sends no Screen Time selections or usage history to Zecro Studios. Read the full Sunda Privacy Policy.
