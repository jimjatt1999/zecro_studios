Welcome to Word Hacker! This privacy policy explains how Zecro Studios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information for the Word Hacker mobile application ("App").

Information We Collect

Word Hacker is designed with privacy in mind. Currently, the App:

  • Does Not Collect Personal Information: We do not collect any information that personally identifies you, such as your name, email address, or location.
  • Stores Data Locally: All game progress, settings, unlocked levels, Breaker Points (BP), and chosen themes are stored exclusively on your device. This data is not transmitted to us or any third party.
  • No User Accounts: The App does not require user accounts.

How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information, we do not use it for any purpose. Locally stored data is used solely to provide the App's functionality, save your progress, and personalize your experience within the App.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information stored on your device, but please be aware that no security measures are perfect or impenetrable. Data stored locally is subject to the security of your device.

Third-Party Services

Word Hacker does not integrate with third-party services that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Word Hacker is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the relevant age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

The initial version focuses on local storage. This policy may be updated if future features (like leaderboards or cloud sync) require data collection, in which case users will be informed.
